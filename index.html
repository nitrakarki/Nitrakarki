About Nitra Hiking Trek

Your journey into the Himalayas starts here.

Nitra Hiking Trek is a Nepal-based hiking and trekking service focused on helping travelers experience the incredible natural beauty, mountains and culture of Nepal.

Led by Nitra Bahadur Karki, Nitra Hiking Trek aims to provide enjoyable and memorable adventures for visitors who want to explore Nepal's Himalayan landscapes.
