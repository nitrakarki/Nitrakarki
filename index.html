Popular Nepal Treks

Explore the beautiful Himalayas with Nitra Hiking Trek.

Everest Base Camp

📅 14 Days · 📍 Everest Region (5,364m)

Spectacular Himalayan views, legendary Sherpa villages, Tengboche Monastery, and the iconic base of Mt. Everest.

Annapurna Base Camp

📅 5 Days · 📍 Annapurna Region (4,130m)

Hike through terraced fields, lush rhododendron forests, and stand surrounded by 360° mountain walls in the Annapurna Sanctuary.

Langtang Valley

📅 10 Days · 📍 Langtang Region (3,870m)

Discover peaceful trails, high glacier views, and the rich Tamang heritage just north of Kathmandu.

Mardi Himal Trek

📅 6 Days · 📍 Annapurna Region (4,500m)

A hidden gem trek offering close-up views of Mount Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) away from crowded main trails.

Manaslu Circuit Trek

📅 14 Days · 📍 Manaslu Region (5,106m)

An epic off-the-beaten-path wilderness circuit around the 8th highest peak in the world crossing Larkya La Pass.

EBC via Gokyo Cho La Pass

📅 17 Days · 📍 Everest Region (5,545m)

A challenging Himalayan adventure combining Gokyo Lakes, Gokyo Ri, Cho La Pass, Everest Base Camp, and Kala Patthar.

How long have you been working in tourism?

I have been actively working in Nepal's tourism and mountain trekking sector since 2014 (12+ years of experience).

Where are you originally from?

I was born and raised in Solukhumbu, Nepal, right in the heart of the Everest Himalayan region.

What major trekking routes do you cover?

I lead treks across all major regions including Everest, Annapurna, Langtang, Mardi Himal, Manaslu, and Kanchenjunga.

Can I customize my trek dates and itinerary?

Yes, absolutely! Contact me directly on WhatsApp to tailor the daily distances and rest days to match your schedule and fitness level.

Are you officially licensed?

Yes, I am a certified trekking guide trained by NATHM and government-licensed in Nepal.

What is the best season for trekking in Nepal?

Autumn (September to November) and Spring (March to May) offer the clearest skies and best weather conditions for high-altitude trekking.
